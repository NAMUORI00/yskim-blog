This blog is a place for notes worth keeping from research, engineering, and knowledge-work projects.

The goal is not to publish only finished conclusions. I want each note to keep enough context to show why a decision was made, what evidence supported it, and what could change it later.

Korean is the primary language for this site. English pages are added when a note benefits from a second-language version.
